Hello, I'm Jan Wehner. I'm an AI Governance Researcher at the [Institute for AI Policy and Strategy (IAPS)](https://www.iaps.ai/), where I work on cyber risks from advanced AI and on safeguards for the use of AI in government. Previously, I was a Winter Fellow at [GovAI](https://www.governance.ai/) under [Alan Chan](https://www.achan.ca/), and I researched AI Safety and Interpretability at the [CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security](https://cispa.de/en), supervised by [Prof. Mario Fritz](https://cispa.de/en/people/mario.fritz) and [Prof. David Krueger](https://davidscottkrueger.com/). My past work spans [Representation Engineering](/publication/representation_engineering), [harmful fine-tuning attacks](/publication/representation_noising) and [Inverse Reinforcement Learning](/publication/explaining_learned_reward_functions).

Please don't hesitate to reach out to me at [jan(at)iaps.ai](mailto:jan@iaps.ai)!
